## Description

Alex's Fantasy Predictions is a free, read-only fantasy football projection
dashboard. Compare weekly NFL player projections across multiple model families,
explore season leaders, view accuracy history, and inspect model comparison data
from fantasy.alexfree.me.

This app is for fantasy football research and entertainment only. It does not
offer betting, contests, real-money gameplay, accounts, subscriptions, or in-app
purchases.

## App Review Notes

No login is required. The app is a read-only client for
`https://fantasy.alexfree.me`. It has no purchases, no sports betting, no
contests, and no real-money gameplay. Analytics are not included in v1.

Remote NFL/ESPN player headshots are not fetched in v1 while content rights are
unverified; the app uses local initials/glyph placeholders instead.
