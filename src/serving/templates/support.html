Alex's Fantasy Predictions

Support

Alex's Fantasy Predictions is a free, read-only fantasy football projections app backed by the public dashboard at fantasy.alexfree.me.

Get Help

  • Report bugs or request fixes through GitHub Issues.
  • If the app shows stale or unavailable data, refresh the screen and check the dashboard at fantasy.alexfree.me.
  • No login, subscription, payment, or fantasy contest account is required to use the app.

Review Notes

The app is for fantasy football research and entertainment. It does not offer betting, contests, real-money gameplay, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.
