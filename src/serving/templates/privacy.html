Alex's Fantasy Predictions

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Alex's Fantasy Predictions is a free, read-only fantasy football projections app and dashboard. The app does not require an account and does not ask you to enter personal information.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration or login data.
  • No payment information or in-app purchases.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data.
  • No third-party analytics SDK.
  • No advertising or cross-app tracking.

Operational Logs

Requests to fantasy.alexfree.me may be processed by standard hosting, load-balancing, and security infrastructure. Those systems may create operational logs such as request time, route, IP address, user agent, and basic error diagnostics. These logs are used to keep the service reliable, investigate abuse, and diagnose outages. They are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

App Functionality

The iPhone app fetches public projection, accuracy, comparison, and documentation data from fantasy.alexfree.me. The app stores user interface preferences and a small offline snapshot cache on your device so the dashboard can reopen quickly.

Changes

If the app later adds analytics, accounts, subscriptions, or other data-collection features, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are shipped.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the support page at fantasy.alexfree.me/support.
