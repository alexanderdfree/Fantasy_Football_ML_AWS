- **What:** The Open item tracked the built-but-unrun attention batch-size + LR ablation harness. The concern was whether larger attention batches could speed local/GPU training without recreating the earlier large-batch accuracy regression, and whether LR should stay fixed, scale by sqrt(batch), or scale linearly.
- **Fix:** Ran the full local CUDA sweep: six positions x eight seeds x five variants, with Ridge sentinel preflight. Zero job errors; ridge sentinel spread was 0.0 for every position. User selected the conservative production policy after reviewing the held-out FP MAE table: apply `b2_lrlin` (2x attention batch + 2x attention LR) to QB/WR/TE/K/DST, and `b2_lrsqrt` (2x attention batch + sqrt(2) LR) to RB because RB's yardage heads regressed slightly at linear LR. Scheduler LR floors/maxima were scaled through attention-only override keys so the regular NN scheduler stays at its prior production value. The batch/LR harness and tune_nn now route scheduler LR-scale params through those attention keys, preserving correct baselines and sampled trial behavior.
- **Lesson:** Batch-size tuning is an optimizer-policy choice, not just a DataLoader knob. For AdamW, double-batch + linear LR worked broadly, but RB showed that yardage heads can be LR-sensitive even when sparse-count heads improve; use paired seed deltas and target-level deltas before hard-pinning a global policy.
