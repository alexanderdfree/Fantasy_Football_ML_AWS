- **What:** the new-sources research (§B2) proposed team Pass-Rate-Over-Expected + neutral pace as the highest-confidence in-house re-sorter for the RB ordering edge experts hold (iso_edge +0.168, calibration-proof). Screened four season-to-date team-week rates (`team_proe_std`, `team_neutral_pass_rate_std`, `team_neutral_sec_per_play_std`, `team_plays_pg_std`) from nflfastR PBP (`pass_oe`/`wp`/snap-gap pace), W-1-lagged, whitelisted into every model + the attention static branch. QB/RB/WR/TE × {baseline, proe_pace} × 3 seeds, judged on rank metrics (hit@12, Spearman, regret@12, position-lineup regret, elite_top24), MAE as the Ridge-sentinel guard.
- **Result — benchmark-flat on every served (learned) head.** The feature *takes* (RB Ridge MAE 4.1807→4.1723, sentinel satisfied — injection + whitelist reach the models), but on the **served RB ranker (LightGBM) every rank metric sits inside the seed band**: hit@12 Δ+0.003 (σ0.009), Spearman Δ+0.001, regret@12 Δ+0.72 (σ1.72), regret@24 Δ+0.27 (σ0.89), MAE flat. The RB attention head is flat-to-slightly-worse (regret@12 +1.6 at ~6σ, a small regression). WR/QB/TE learned heads are likewise flat/mixed. The only consistent signal is on **Ridge deep-lineup regret** (RB regret@24 −3.76, WR −0.76/−1.60, QB −0.63, all deterministic σ=0) — i.e. team-volume helps the *linear* head's deep-tier ordering slightly, but Ridge is not the served skill-position ranker and its top-12 regret simultaneously worsens (RB +1.72). Net: no decision-relevant ordering gain on the heads that serve RB/WR.
- **Lesson:** mirrors the draft-capital precedent — a feature can be *real* (moves the deterministic Ridge fit, so it's genuinely in the data path) yet **benchmark-flat because the gain lands only on a non-served head / non-decision slice**. The RB ordering edge experts hold is not recovered by team-level pass-volume tendency; the signal that would move the served LightGBM/attention RB ranker is not team pass-rate. Screen the served head on the decision metric (regret/hit@k), not just "did Ridge move." The `ab_proe_pace` spec + team-week PBP builder are retained as reusable tooling; the feature is **not** wired into production configs.
