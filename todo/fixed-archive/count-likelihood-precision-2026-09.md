### [FIXED] Hurdle count likelihoods lost probability mass and gradient direction at low positive rates
- **File(s):** [src/shared/training.py](../../src/shared/training.py) (`negbin2_log_prob`, `ztnb2_log_prob`, `ztp_log_prob`), [src/shared/count_math.py](../../src/shared/count_math.py) (new), [src/analysis/verify_count_likelihoods.py](../../src/analysis/verify_count_likelihoods.py) (new CUDA acceptance probe), `tests/shared/test_count_likelihood_precision.py`, `tests/analysis/test_verify_count_likelihoods.py`. Isolated from PR #1565 (`codex/audit-runtime-correctness` @ `7af61481`, commit `c1df190d`) as a HELD draft; finding `COUNT-PRECISION` in that branch's `benchmark_history/audits/2026-09-10-model-numerical-regressions.json`, reproduced against `e3ed317f`.
- **What:** The zero-truncated NB-2 log-pmf was `log P_NB(k) - log1p(-P_NB(0))` with `P_NB(0)` clamped to `1 - 1e-7`, and `log P_NB(0) = r * (log r - log(r + mu))` was a difference of nearly equal FP32 logs (`r = 1/alpha`). At the `GatedHead` output floor `mu ≈ 2e-6` with `log_alpha = -5` (`r ≈ 148`), `log(r + mu)` rounds to `log r`, `P_NB(0)` rounds to exactly one, the clamp floors the survival mass at `1e-7` instead of the true `≈ 2e-6`, and the conditional probability of a single reception exceeds one while the clamped survival term contributes no gradient. The audit recorded "conditional P1=16.7771969; NLL mu gradient=-499998.9375" against the expected "P1=.9999989933; NLL mu gradient=+.503369" — the NLL pushed the rate *up* toward a near-certain observation of one instead of down. `ztp_log_prob` carried the same clamped `log1p(-exp(-mu))` survival term, and under FP16/BF16 autocast the whole computation ran in the reduced dtype. Incidence per the audit: "No invalid P1 in 18 existing RB/WR/TE default test parameter frames; finite-domain boundary proof, not observed default-cohort incidence."
- **Fix:** `count_math.py` supplies tensor-only primitives: `_count_loss_inputs` promotes FP16/BF16 inputs to FP32 while preserving FP64 callers (a production no-op because CUDA training runs FP32/TF32); `_log_exprel`, `_log1p_div_minus_one` and `_nb2_zero_mass_terms` are series-guarded near zero so both `torch.where` branches stay finite in value and gradient. `_nb2_log_prob` writes the conditional `P(1 | Y > 0)` as `exprel(-r) / exprel(z)` with `r = log1p(alpha * mu)` and `z = r / alpha = -log P_NB(0)`, and `_nb2_log_positive_ratio` evaluates `log(P(y) / P(1))` through an eight-step `log1p` recurrence plus a six-term Stirling remainder on `Gamma(b + n) / Gamma(b)`, `b = 1 + 1/alpha`, combining the Poisson-limit terms analytically so `log(alpha)` cannot cancel its own gradient. `ztp_log_prob` becomes `-log_exprel(mu) + (y - 1) * log(mu) - lgamma(y + 1)`. No probability clamp, no count cap, fixed tensor shapes (vmap/CUDA-graph compatible); `y = 0` returns `-inf` from the truncated families and `-z` from `negbin2_log_prob`. Same distributions, same head parameters and checkpoint shapes; `hurdle_negbin_value_loss`, `hurdle_poisson_value_loss` and their `_capturable` variants are unchanged callers.
- **Validation:** `tests/shared/test_count_likelihood_precision.py` checks values and parameter gradients against a 90-digit `Decimal` oracle: FP32/FP64, truncated and untruncated NB over 19 parameter rows (including `alpha = e^90` and `y = 1000`), ZTP, normalization over 4097 counts, FP16/BF16 promotion, eager-vs-capturable and vmap identity, native MPS, and CUDA graph capture when available; the existing `tests/shared/test_losses.py` controls pass unchanged. Model-output effect is confined to the `hurdle_negbin` value loss on RB/WR/TE `receptions` (`poisson_nll` heads use `torch.nn.PoissonNLLLoss`; K, DST and QB have no hurdle heads). The change stays HELD behind the dual-metric + protected-cohort gate (`todo/model-default-repair/README.md`): the 2026-09-17 repair campaign's separate FP64 numerical variant reached a 4.63e-8 maximum scaled value/gradient discrepancy but did not satisfy the forecast gate (`todo/model-default-repair/results.md`), and no paired forecast comparison of this rewrite exists yet.
- **Lesson:** A probability clamp hides a cancellation instead of fixing it — `clamp(max=1-1e-7)` turned a rounding failure in `log(r) - log(r + mu)` into a silent wrong-signed gradient. Write survival and conditional terms in the log domain (`log1p`, `exprel`, gamma-ratio recurrences) so the small quantities are computed directly, keep AMP count arithmetic in FP32, and test against an independent high-precision oracle at the head's actual output floor (`mu = softplus + 1e-6`), not only at comfortable parameter values.
