### [FIXED] QB `prior_season_mean_qbr_total` / `pts_added` skipped the leak-safe train-mean fill and zero-filled
- **File(s):** [src/qb/features.py](../../src/qb/features.py) (`fill_nans`), [tests/qb/test_features.py](../../tests/qb/test_features.py). audit-1499 Tier C-qbfill PR; issue #1521.
- **What:** Both prior-season rate features are whitelisted in `INCLUDE_FEATURES["prior_season"]` but absent from `specific_features` — the column set the pipeline passes to `fill_nans` — and QB's `fill_nans` was a bare wrapper, so they fell through to `build_position_features`' catch-all `fillna(0)`. ESPN Total QBR is a 0–100 rate (train mean 54.4, sd 13.1): 24.1 % of QB train rows (1,679 / 6,961 on the 2026-09-17 sealed release) read as QBR 0, z ≈ −4.15 post-scaler, and 564 of them had played the prior season (they merely lack an ESPN QBR record), so the "rookie placeholder" reading did not hold. `pts_added` shares the identical NaN mask (z ≈ −0.31). Same class as RB #390, WR #1368, TE #1290, DST #856.
- **Fix:** Mirror the WR/TE carve-out: append the two columns to the train-only-mean fill list when present and not already in `qb_feature_cols`; `prior_season_games_played` deliberately stays zero-filled (it is the rookie sentinel). Tests pin the fill, the config wiring (whitelisted, not specific) and the no-op when the columns are absent. QB Batch benchmark evidence is recorded in the PR.
- **Lesson:** The rate-vs-volume fill convention has now been re-applied at five positions. A whitelisted-but-not-specific rate feature is a structural gap the whitelist itself should surface (derive the carve-out as `prior_season` rates ∖ `specific_features`, #1383); until then, every new `prior_season_mean_*` rate needs a `fill_nans` entry at the position that consumes it, and a rookie placeholder is only neutral for volume aggregates.
