### [FIXED] LGBM Optuna tuning now uses leased core pooling and 3-seed trial scoring
- **File(s):** [../src/tuning/tune_lgbm.py](../../src/tuning/tune_lgbm.py) (`--seeds`, seed-versioned studies, core-pool lifecycle, seed-averaged objective/comparison), [../tests/tuning/test_tune_lgbm.py](../../tests/tuning/test_tune_lgbm.py) (seed parsing, storage names, leased `n_jobs`, fold-level pruning, comparison aggregation), [../SETUP.md](../../SETUP.md) and [../.github/workflows/retune-lgbm.yml](../../.github/workflows/retune-lgbm.yml) (operator defaults).
- **What:** The old Open item noted that `study.optimize(n_jobs=...)` existed but was effectively conservative and had to be coordinated manually with `LGBM_N_JOBS` to avoid `n_jobs x per-trial-threads` oversubscription. The tuner also judged each trial at a single LightGBM seed (`42`), so borderline wins could be seed artifacts.
- **Fix:** `tune_lgbm` now defaults to seeds `42,43,44`, optimizes mean CV MAE across folds and seeds, reports holdout old-vs-tuned mean±std, and stores studies under seed-versioned names such as `tune_lgbm_seedavg_v1_s42-43-44_rb.db`. Parallel local runs self-start the existing `src.shared.core_pool` coordinator and lease LightGBM thread counts per fit; `--no-core-pool` or coordinator failure falls back to the existing `LGBM_N_JOBS=1` parallel-trial guard.
- **Lesson:** Trial-level parallelism and per-model tree threads must be controlled by one allocator, not by stacked defaults. When a tuner objective changes from single-seed to seed-averaged, version the study storage so old and new objective values cannot be compared or resumed accidentally.
