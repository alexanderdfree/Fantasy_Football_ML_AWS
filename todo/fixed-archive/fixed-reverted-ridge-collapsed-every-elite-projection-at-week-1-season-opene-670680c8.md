### [FIXED → REVERTED] Ridge collapsed every elite projection at Week 1 (season opener) on the upcoming-week board
- **File(s):** [../src/features/engineer.py](../../src/features/engineer.py) (rolling/EWMA/trend regrouped by `player_id` alone; `opp_def_*_L5` / `opp_def_pts_allowed_L5` / `opp_*_pts_allowed_to_pos` regrouped by team/opponent alone), [../src/data/external_sources.py](../../src/data/external_sources.py) (`derive_active_contracts` drops `year_signed==0`), [../tests/test_engineer.py](../../tests/test_engineer.py) + [../tests/test_external_sources.py](../../tests/test_external_sources.py). PR #1109.
- **What:** On the upcoming-week (always Week 1) board the **Ridge** column under-predicted elite RB/WR/TE by ~10 FP (Jahmyr Gibbs ridge 9.4 vs NN/attn/LGBM consensus ~19.6); QB fine; values present, not null. **Not a 2026 regression and not a data bug:** the recent-form windows (`rolling_*`/`ewma_*`/`trend_*`) and opponent-defense rolling (`opp_def_*`) were grouped **within-season**, so they are **0 at every season opener** (confirmed on the real 2025 split — Week-1 rolling/opp_def = 0, populated from Week 2). Ridge leans on them and amplifies a coordinated all-zeros shift through its **PCA**, collapsing every elite toward the mean; severity tracks `ridge_pca_components` (**RB-80 ≫ WR-30 ≫ TE/QB none**). NN/LGBM lean on `prior_season_*` and were unaffected. Proven pre-existing: ridge collapses elite RBs identically at **2025** Week 1 in the deployed cache (Gibbs 9.0 vs 18.1) and recovers by Week 2–3.
- **Fix:** Group the recent-form windows by `player_id` alone (and opp-defense by team/opponent alone) so a player's (and a defense's) trailing form **carries across the offseason AND across trades** — a prior-season average beats 0. Leakage-safe via `shift(1)` on the (player_id, season, week)-sorted frame. Team-relative SHARE features (target/carry/air-yards share, `snap_pct` static lag) stay stint-aware (#666/#677). Also drops OTC `year_signed==0` placeholders so `contract_age` can't be ≈ season for a rostered fringe player fed through the live serving contract override. Local RB retrain: Week-1 top-20 ridge gap **−9 → −3.5** (Gibbs 9→12.5, Saquon→19, Henry→19); weeks 2–18 stable; overall RB ridge MAE 4.19.
- **Lesson:** "Ridge broken for 2026" was ridge being structurally weak at *every* season opener, exposed by a board that only ever shows Week 1 — **slice by week before calling an aggregate "fine"** (the all-weeks 2025 cache hid it). Within-season rolling reset is a deliberate design (#722 stint-awareness) but it zeroes the opener; for a **linear+PCA** model that coordinated all-zeros shift dominates the PCA basis and is catastrophic, while tree/NN models fall back on `prior_season_*`. Empty history is not zero ability — carry recent form across the boundary. The static `opp_def_*_L5` columns and the attention opp-defense *sequence* (`build_opp_defense_history_arrays`) are **separate representations** — the sequence stays season-isolated by design (`test_season_boundary_isolation`); changing the static columns does not touch it. (Diagnostics in `src/analysis/` that key season openers off `rolling==0` now mis-detect them — follow-up.)
- **Post-merge measurement (2026-06-11):** the carryover's one cost is QB `passing_yards` — LGBM +0.10 FP MAE (Week-1-concentrated: wk1 ~67 vs wk2+ ~56, i.e. the trade-off zone where formerly-zero features now carry prior-season form) and plain-NN +0.2–0.35 FP (systematic across 3 seeds, ~59→62–64 passing-yards MAE, NOT Week-1-concentrated); ridge improved on every skill position and attention was flat. Accepted with the merge (values stayed within the week's historical band); revisit only with a QB-NN retune on the new feature distribution, judged per the stop rules.
- **REVERTED (2026-06-11, same day, PR #1137):** owner-directed revert back to the per-season/stint grouping after a same-code isolation A/B (carry vs season-reset, seed 42): the carried prior-season form misleads QB openers (NN passing_yards 62.4→56.7, LGBM 66.9→64.2 under reset; worst on year-over-year changers — C.Williams/Lawrence/Maye), though reset also measured QB-LGBM FP +0.101 / ridge +0.024 WORSE with the full current feature set. The `year_signed==0` contract clamp from #1109 was kept. **The Week-1 Ridge board collapse this entry describes is live again** — a future re-fix needs a Week-1-specific treatment (games-gap companion feature so models can discount carried form, or ridge-side imputation), not a blanket carry into every model.
- **REVERT COMPLETED (2026-06-20, PR #1214 / audit #1199):** #1137's revert missed one column — `opp_def_pts_allowed_L5` kept grouping by `team` alone (cross-season), inconsistent with the per-season grouping #1137 restored for its `opp_def_*_L5` siblings. #1214 regroups it by `(team, season)` so it resets at each season boundary too. **Benchmark-flat:** the deterministic **Ridge MAE data-identity tell is flat to ±0.002** on the affected offensive positions (QB/RB/WR/TE) and **0.0000 on K/DST** (opp_def is an offensive *matchup* feature, absent from DST/K) — the reset only touches the early-season rows where the L5 window previously bled across the boundary; the larger NN swings (QB nn −0.16, RB/WR nn ≈ −0.05) were single-seed noise, not signal. Validated via an isolated Batch benchmark (`39560e5` image) vs main `6502866` before merge.
