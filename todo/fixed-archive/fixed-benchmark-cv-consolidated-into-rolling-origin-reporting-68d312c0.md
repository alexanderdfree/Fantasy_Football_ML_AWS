### [FIXED] `benchmark --cv` consolidated into rolling-origin reporting
- **File(s):** [../src/benchmarking/benchmark.py](../../src/benchmarking/benchmark.py) and [../src/benchmarking/parallel_train.py](../../src/benchmarking/parallel_train.py) (`--cv` parser help + CLI dispatch now route benchmark-history publishers to rolling-origin), [../tests/test_benchmark_rolling_origin.py](../../tests/test_benchmark_rolling_origin.py) and [../tests/test_parallel_train.py](../../tests/test_parallel_train.py) (CLI alias coverage), [../SETUP.md](../../SETUP.md) / [../scripts/train-local-parallel.sh](../../scripts/train-local-parallel.sh) (operator docs), [../docs/adr/0001-temporal-split.md](../../docs/adr/0001-temporal-split.md) / [../docs/adr/CHANGELOG.md](../../docs/adr/CHANGELOG.md) (D1 update).
- **What:** The local benchmark CLIs exposed two overlapping multi-season reporting modes. `--cv` ran `run_cv_pipeline` over expanding-window folds where the validation season was also the reported evaluation season (`val == eval`) and stopped at 2024. `--rolling-origin` was already the cleaner walk-forward superset: per origin train `[..T-2]`, validate on `T-1`, test on `T`, include 2025, and keep the final origin comparable to the production split. Keeping both benchmark-history publishers' `--cv` flags on the older path invited users to headline the older optimistic subset.
- **Fix:** `benchmark --cv` and `parallel_train --cv` are now deprecated aliases for rolling-origin reporting. Each CLI computes one `rolling_origin_mode = args.rolling_origin or args.cv`, prints a one-line deprecation note for bare `--cv`, calls `run_rolling_origin`, prints the rolling-origin table, and writes `entry["mode"] = "rolling_origin"` into benchmark history. The lower-level expanding-window CV machinery remains intact for direct per-position/ad-hoc callers and for tuning flows where validation-as-objective is intentional.
- **Lesson:** Keep operator-facing benchmark flags aligned with the methodology they are meant to headline. A statistically older internal diagnostic can stay available, but benchmark-history publishing paths should route to the leakage-clean walk-forward evaluator so history rows and local commands do not encode competing definitions of "multi-season benchmark."
