### [FIXED] Expert forecasts lost scoring components outside the model target list

- **File(s):** `src/data/nflcom_loader.py`, `src/serving/{expert_sources,core,espn_projections,upcoming_week,comparison,timeline}.py`, `src/analysis/{analysis_nflcom_baseline,analysis_expert_comparison,build_comparison_summary}.py`; original `51076bc73` (PR #1550), consolidated with #1573/#1574.
- **What:** NFL.com ingestion discarded WR/TE rushing and QB receiving. Deebo Samuel's archived 2025 Week 3 full forecast consequently displayed 13.174 PPR instead of 15.625, losing 2.451 rushing points. Restoring full forecasts alone would also make comparison errors unfair when actuals intentionally include only model-projected components.
- **Fix:** Preserve complete normalized offensive stats and full display forecasts. Store separate per-format expert comparison totals, and use the shared-component scorer at serving, Timeline and offline metric boundaries. Keep the `shared_components_v3` reference recipe from ADR-0024; do not rank it with full forecasts. Invalidate truncated source caches, bump serving cache schema to 11 and update the upcoming scoring signature. Tests retain the complete Deebo forecast while comparing 13.174 shared-component points and reject missing-component/full-total fallbacks.
- **Lesson:** Source ingestion must preserve information; metric calculations must explicitly choose the same scoring components on both sides. A display forecast and a comparable forecast are different contracts.
