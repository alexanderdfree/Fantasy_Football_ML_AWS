### [FIXED] Expert forecasts lost scoring components outside the model target list

- **File(s):** `src/data/nflcom_loader.py`, `src/serving/{expert_sources,core,espn_projections,upcoming_week}.py`, `src/analysis/{analysis_nflcom_baseline,analysis_expert_comparison}.py`, `src/shared/evaluation_cohorts.py` (PR pending).
- **What:** PR #1541 aligned comparison actuals and player-weeks but left expert forecasts restricted to model heads. NFL.com ingestion also dropped WR/TE rushing and QB receiving. Deebo Samuel's archived 2025 Week 3 row scored 13.174 PPR instead of 15.625: 13.71 rushing yards plus 0.18 rushing TDs were lost (2.451 points). RotoWire, ESPN and FFToday totals had the same position-dependent omission. Existing cached totals and reference ranks could survive a scorer-only fix.
- **Fix:** Preserve the complete normalized offensive stat set, use one expert offensive scorer in serving and offline evaluation, and retain K/DST rules and model target lists. Bump NFL.com and ESPN source caches, serving schema, upcoming scoring signature, and the pregame reference recipe; preserve old recipe rows as history. Regression tests exercise all offensive positions/formats/projectors, source normalization, Deebo's row, sparse stats, and full-score reference ranking.
- **Lesson:** Matching actuals and player IDs does not establish fair expert scoring. Trace source ingestion through normalized caches, forecast aggregation, serialized totals and cohort selection. A model's prediction heads cannot serve as an external source's scoring whitelist.
