### [FIXED] RB team-HHI L3 rollings not stint-aware — a traded RB inherited the old team's backfield concentration (the missed second half of #1192/#1193)
- **File(s):** [../src/rb/features.py](../../src/rb/features.py) (`team_rb_carry_hhi_L3` / `team_rb_target_hhi_L3`), [../tests/rb/test_features.py](../../tests/rb/test_features.py), audit issue #1425, 2026-07-05 solve-issues tier-C-high PR.
- **What:** PR #1385 made RB's team-**share** L3 rollings stint-aware (#1192/#1193) but its commit scoped itself to the share denominators and missed the two adjacent team-**HHI** (Herfindahl concentration) L3 rollings, which stayed grouped on `['player_id','season']` only. So a traded RB's `team_rb_{carry,target}_hhi_L3` averaged the OLD team's per-game backfield concentration into the first ≤3 games of the new stint — both are production include-features feeding Ridge/LGBM/NN training and serving. On the KC-2RB→SF-solo trade fixture the buggy grouping yields wk5/6/7 HHI `0.5/0.6667/0.8333` where stint-aware yields `NaN/1.0/1.0`.
- **Fix:** Roll the two `game_*_hhi` cols over the same stint-aware grouping as the shares (`['player_id','season','stint_id']`; `stint_id` is row-order-identical after the 1-to-1 merge), mirroring the share-rolling pattern at `features.py:132-138`. Positive-controlled against pre-fix code on the trade fixture. `opportunity_index_L3` (same file, same grouping class) has the identical latent flaw and is left as a scoped follow-up. Runtime features → RB retrain only; a small real benchmark delta is expected, concentrated on the rare post-trade-stint rows (Ridge MAE moved +0.007 in a local eager smoke — the data-identity tell that the inputs genuinely changed).
- **Lesson:** A "same class as an already-fixed bug" follow-up (WR #674 → RB/TE #1192/#1193 → this) recurs on *sibling columns in the same file* when the original fix was scoped to one column family — when you make a rolling stint-aware, grep the whole file for every other rolling grouped on `['player_id','season']` and convert them together, or the reviewer/audit finds the leftover months later.
