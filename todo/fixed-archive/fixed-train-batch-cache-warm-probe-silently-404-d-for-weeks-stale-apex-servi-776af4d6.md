### [FIXED] train-batch cache-warm probe silently 404'd for weeks — stale apex SERVICE_URL
- **File(s):** [../.github/workflows/train-batch.yml](../../.github/workflows/train-batch.yml) (`SERVICE_URL` → `https://fantasy.alexfree.me`). PR #1131.
- **What:** The Flask app moved to the `fantasy` subdomain (deploy.yml line ~61 documents the split; the apex is a separate personal site), but train-batch's post-train "Warm serving cache" probe kept the apex URL. Every probe 404'd; the step is deliberately best-effort (`continue-on-error`), so nothing went red — and the serving containers' S3 predictions cache (`models/predictions_cache/predictions.parquet`) sat stale at Jun-8 through ~10 retrains. Found only when the #1106 cohort validation needed a fresh cache. Residual (pre-existing, D14): even on the right host, `/warm` 502s when the recompute exceeds the gateway window after heavy artifact churn — an ECS force-new-deployment (boot-path recompute) is the recovery; the airtight fix is the ADR-0014 "hydrate the pre-uploaded cache in-flight" follow-up.
- **Lesson:** A best-effort step that can silently 404 forever needs at least one loud signal (assert the HTTP code or alert on N consecutive failures), and env constants duplicated across workflows (deploy.yml had the right URL, train-batch.yml the stale one) drift — grep all workflows when a domain/endpoint moves.
