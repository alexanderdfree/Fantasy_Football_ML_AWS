### [FIXED] NextWeek NFL.com column rendered as a wall of `--` off-season — expert columns were pair-gated, not per-source
- **File(s):** [../src/serving/frontend/src/views/NextWeek.jsx](../../src/serving/frontend/src/views/NextWeek.jsx) (+ regenerated `static/js/app.js` bundle), PR #1483.
- **What:** With the artifact serving 2026 Week 1 (off-season), `nflcom_pred` was `null` for all 766 rows — the hvpkod/NFL-Data mirror publishes weekly CSVs in-season only, so `2026/1/projected/*.csv` 404s, `load_nflcom_with_gsis_id` raises, and the optional-expert guard in `upcoming_week.py` degrades to null **by design** (refresh workflow green, artifact fresh — not a pipeline failure). But RotoWire rides Sleeper's live API, which already serves 2026 projections, and the frontend's `hasExperts` gate was an OR across both sources — so RotoWire's presence kept the empty NFL.com column visible as a full column of `fmt(null)` → `--`. Third distinct cause of the recurring "NFL.com column empty" symptom (after the numpy.int64-season nflreadpy reject and the 2027-roster-request bugs, both archived below).
- **Fix:** Gate each expert column on its own data presence: `expertHasData`, derived from `COLUMNS`' `expert: true` entries (single-source registry — a future third expert source can't be silently hidden by a stale hardcoded key list), applied to both the table visibility filter and the Columns picker. Verified both ways against the real S3 artifact (column hidden at 0 non-null; reappears with one injected value). Self-heals: NFL.com returns automatically when its 2026 projections publish (~Sept).
- **Lesson:** Gating a column *group* on ANY member having data turns a single-source outage into a visible wall of `--` indistinguishable from a broken loader — feature-detect per source. And off-season `nflcom_pred` all-null is **expected**: before suspecting the loader (or re-fixing the two archived causes above), curl the upstream `{year}/{week}/projected/{POS}_projected.csv` — a 404 means the data doesn't exist yet, not that the code broke.
