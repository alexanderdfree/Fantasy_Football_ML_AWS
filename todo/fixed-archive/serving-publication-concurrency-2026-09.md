### [FIXED] Concurrent model/cache publication and serving recovery failures

**File(s):** `src/shared/artifact_publication.py`, `artifact_gc.py`, `model_sync.py`, `prediction_cache.py`; `src/batch/train.py`, `launch.py`, `benchmark.py`; `src/scripts/promote.py`, `register_training_source.py`, `build_serving_cache.py`, `seed_s3_models.py`; `src/serving/core.py`, `routes.py`; Batch/EC2/deploy workflows and `infra/aws/` (PR pending).

**What:** The September 10 serving/infra audit reproduced stale cleanup deleting a concurrent stable artifact, older training replacing newer models, mixed prediction/metric/fingerprint cache uploads, shared data failures returning healthy status, and failed-position sentinels being ignored by aggregate requests. Bootstrap also replaced the working task policy with grants insufficient to read serving data.

**Fix:** Manifest v3 isolates releases from queued legacy writers, verifies actual image ancestry and conditionally publishes. GC deletes only CAS-retired immutable keys; explicit rollback copies to a fresh key and preserves source order. Cache members and snapshot form one checksummed immutable generation and one S3 bundle; deploy prepares a valid bundle before ECS mutation. Shared initialization failure participates in health, hydration/retry clears it, and aggregate refresh checks include failed attempts. Bootstrap preserves scoped read/list/cache-write grants and validates manifest-backed model artifacts before changes.

**Validation:** Regression suites exercise all six publication/recovery paths, out-of-order and conflicting publishers, delayed cleanup, rollback races, mixed cache writers, readers spanning pointer changes, malformed bundles, initialization recovery, and deployment preparation ordering. A read-only live migration rehearsal verified all 30 retained model archives carry compatible full source revisions; simulated migration preserved their bytes. Final suite and benchmark evidence are recorded in the PR.

**Lesson:** Atomic operations on individual files or objects do not form a transaction across a generation. Publication order must follow verified source lineage, cleanup must not sweep another publisher's uploads, and health must distinguish a failed attempt from a cold start. Protocol changes must include old queued writers, operator CLIs and first-deploy preparation. See ADR-0011 and ADR-0014 for the active contracts.
