### [FIXED] WR/TE opportunity and red-zone share windows crossed mid-season team stints
- **File(s):** [src/wr/features.py](../../src/wr/features.py), [src/te/features.py](../../src/te/features.py) (`_compute_features`: the `opportunity_index_L3` / `redzone_target_share_L3` rolling forms); tests in [tests/test_receiver_team_stints.py](../../tests/test_receiver_team_stints.py). Isolated from #1565 (`codex/audit-runtime-correctness` @ 7af61481), where it was one finding of the 2026-09-10 model/data audit reproduced against `0f0fec55`. Fixes audit findings #1499 (WR) and #1500 (TE); merged only after the dual-metric + protected-cohort gate (`todo/model-default-repair/README.md`), whose evidence is attached to PR #1607.
- **What:** Both features are team-relative shares (player weighted opportunity over the team's, player red-zone targets over the team's) whitelisted for WR and TE in the position-`specific` group, which feeds Ridge, LightGBM and the plain NN (it is deliberately not an `ATTN_STATIC` category — the rolling stop-rule — so the attention NN never sees them). They were rolled with `rolling_agg(..., [player_id, season], window=3, agg="mean")`, so a receiver traded mid-season carried up to three games of OLD-team share into the first NEW-team weeks — a share of a denominator that no longer exists. The neighbouring team-share window (`team_wr_target_share_L3` / `team_te_target_share_L3`, #674 / #1385) already reset per stint via the locally built `stint_id`, and RB's `opportunity_index_L3` became stint-aware in #1474; WR/TE were the remaining gap.
- **Fix:** Reuse the existing `stint_grp = [player_id, season, stint_id]`: copy the two per-game source columns onto the stint-labelled `df_merged`, roll with `rolling_agg(df_merged, source, stint_grp, window=3, agg="mean")`, and assign back positionally (`df_merged` is the order-preserving left merge of `df`). Raw personal volume (`redzone_targets_L3`) keeps its player-season window on purpose — a player's own red-zone usage stays informative after a trade — and the per-game attention inputs (`game_opportunity_index`, `game_target_share`) are unchanged. Feature names, the whitelists and the attention allowlists are untouched, so training/inference parity holds by construction; `src/wr/features.py` and `src/te/features.py` are not `DATA_PRODUCER_PATHS` (only the `config.py` files are), so the merge seals no new data release.
- **Validation:** `tests/test_receiver_team_stints.py` (10 tests, WR and TE parametrised): shares restart on each stint (NaN on the first game of a new stint, partial means after), same-team frames keep their previous values, row-permutation and non-default-index invariance, season-boundary reset, and a current-game edit cannot rewrite earlier windows; `tests/wr` + `tests/te` otherwise unchanged. Blast radius from the release splits (`data/splits/{train,val,test}.parquet`, read-only; main's builder vs this branch's on identical inputs): WR — 137 team-change rows in 131 player-seasons out of 33,538 WR rows; 352 rows lie in the first three games of a later stint; 320 rows change (137 become NaN, the rest partial new-team means), all inside that reach, plus 40 rows that differ by at most 1.2e-16 (pandas' rolling running sum restarts at the stint boundary). TE — 61 team-change rows in 58 player-seasons out of 19,792; 145 reach rows; 113 semantic changes (61 NaN) plus 10 rounding-only rows. No other column differs. Single-seed CPU eager smoke (seed 42, same uncapped environment on both sides, not evidence): WR — Ridge MAE 4.117 → 4.117, RMSE 5.613 → 5.612; LightGBM 4.034 → 4.014, RMSE 5.502 → 5.495; NN 3.956 → 3.949, RMSE 5.500 → 5.506; attention NN 3.943 → 3.943, RMSE 5.555 → 5.555. TE — Ridge 2.852 → 2.852, RMSE 4.457 → 4.457; LightGBM 2.949 → 2.933, RMSE 4.428 → 4.428; NN 2.828 → 2.818, RMSE 4.561 → 4.560; attention NN 2.850 → 2.850, RMSE 4.542 → 4.542 (main baseline 12da0f92 `benchmark_history/2026-09-18T08-46-32_12da0f92.json` in the shared baseline checkout vs `benchmark_history/2026-09-18T08-51-07_c0fb9a99.json` here; metrics are stored at three decimals). Artifact comparison (64 files): Ridge coefficients/PCA/scalers, every LightGBM booster and the plain-NN weights differ, and the NN scaler differs in exactly the two changed feature means (2 of 110 WR, 2 of 108 TE entries); the attention NN is bit-identical on both positions (50/50 and 57/57 tensors equal, identical attention scalers), so the change is metric-bearing for Ridge, LightGBM and the plain NN, not the attention branch. Protected cohorts, single seed: WR `elite_top24` (n=325) Ridge MAE 6.4129 → 6.4131, NN 6.4344 → 6.4651; WR `weekly_reference_top24` (n=432) Ridge 6.4855 → 6.4855, NN 6.4634 → 6.4925; TE `elite_top24` (n=337) Ridge 5.0654 → 5.0659, NN 4.9951 → 4.9950; TE `weekly_reference_top24` (n=432) Ridge 5.0712 → 5.0719, NN 5.0789 → 5.0774.
- **Lesson:** Sharpens the sibling-window rule already recorded for [RB team-HHI (3b3d79de)](fixed-rb-team-hhi-l3-rollings-not-stint-aware-a-traded-rb-inherited-the-old-3b3d79de.md) and [RB/TE team shares (ff783811)](fixed-rb-te-team-share-l3-rollings-not-stint-aware-mid-season-trades-mixed-t-ff783811.md): the criterion is a team-scoped denominator — a team-relative share is undefined across a team change even when the personal-volume window beside it is not. Rebuilding the feature frame with both builders on the release splits bounds a feature change's blast radius in seconds — do that before spending a benchmark, and keep the benchmark environment identical on both sides (thread caps change torch CPU reductions).
