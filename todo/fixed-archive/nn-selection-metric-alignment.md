### [FIXED] Fantasy-point RMSE selection could not be compared against the raw-stat selectors (machinery added; defaults unchanged)

**Files:** `src/shared/model_selection.py` (new), `src/shared/training.py`, `src/shared/models.py`, `src/shared/pipeline.py`, `src/shared/position_config.py`, `src/shared/position_pipeline.py`, `src/shared/aggregate_targets.py`, `src/tuning/ab_checkpoint_metric.py`, `src/tuning/ab_classical_selection.py`. Ported from PR #1568 (`codex/rmse-checkpoint-selection` @ `1a15bdc1`), which also carried the production default flips and the tuning-objective changes that this port holds back.

**What:** The NN trainer, the Ridge alpha search and LightGBM early stopping could only select on raw-stat criteria (loss-weighted validation MAE, independent per-target CV MAE, per-head validation loss) while reporting scores aggregated PPR fantasy-point error. No matched comparison of selectors was possible, and no selection provenance survived into histories, Batch metrics or saved artifacts.

**Fix (this port):** Add the selectors as opt-in configuration with every production default unchanged: `nn_selection_metric="weighted_mae"` reproduces the previous early-stopping rule exactly (`weighted_rmse` and `fantasy_rmse_ppr` are alternatives; the plateau scheduler and the pruning `epoch_callback` still consume `avg_val_loss`); `ridge_selection_metric="raw_mae"` keeps the previous independent search byte-for-byte (`fantasy_rmse_ppr` runs a bounded joint coordinate search over cached out-of-fold candidates with fixed special heads included); `lgbm_selection_metric="per_target"` keeps the previous callbacks and artifact layout (`fantasy_rmse_ppr` fits the full tree budget, selects per-head prefixes jointly on validation PPR RMSE and persists `selected_iterations`). Histories gain per-target `val_rmse_*`, `val_{mae,rmse}_weighted`, `val_fantasy_{rmse,mae}_{ppr,half_ppr,standard}` and a `checkpoint_selection` block; joint Ridge/LightGBM runs record `selection_info`. Legacy artifacts keep main's exact file and metadata layout. An RB production run (CPU eager, seed 42) on the port is bit-identical to `origin/main` for every saved model, scaler and booster and for the benchmark results.

**Evidence for holding the defaults:** the 36-cell L4/A10G readiness A/B at `s3://ff-predictor-training/ab_runs/rmse-readiness-20260911/full-rmse-e836b79/` shows the attention NN worse for QB and WR on MAE, RMSE and both protected cohorts in 3/3 seeds under PPR-RMSE selection. Any flip needs that dual-metric + protected-cohort gate on matched multi-seed runs (ADR-0002 changelog, 2026-09-18).

**Lesson:** Land selection machinery and its provenance separately from the policy flip, defaulting to the incumbent rule, so the flip can be judged on matched runs. An aligned metric is not evidence of improved held-out accuracy.

**Tuning objective (follow-up port, PR pending):** `tune_nn` (eager, stacked and history scopes) and `tune_lgbm` now optimize validation PPR fantasy-point RMSE, forcing `nn_selection_metric` / `selection_metric="fantasy_rmse_ppr"` per trial, in the fresh namespaces `scheduler_v3_fp_rmse_ppr_v1*`, `history_v3_fp_rmse_ppr_v1*` and `lgbm_ppr_rmse_v1*` (the `scheduler_v2*`/`scheduler_v3*`, `history_v2*`/`history_v3*` and `lgbm_seedavg_v1*` studies are orphaned, not migrated). The trainer's pruning `epoch_callback` reports the selection score instead of the mixed validation loss (production-inert: the callback is `None` outside the tuner and the plateau scheduler still steps on `avg_val_loss`; the RB identity benchmark was re-run on the port), the stacked per-epoch report is `stacked_val_rmse` (each member's PPR RMSE pooled over every validation row, then member-averaged; it replaces the sample-weighted combined loss `stacked_val_losses` of the validation-reduction fix), and `retune-nn-batch.yml` aggregates the per-position namespaces `launch_tune` submits (`aggregate_results --batch-cuda-graph`) instead of a hard-coded `scheduler_v2_mps[_graph]` that the stacked/full-graph defaults never wrote. Production selectors stay legacy.
