- **File(s):** `src/analysis/cuda_graph_gradscale.py` (paired graph/eager harness), [src/shared/training.py](../../src/shared/training.py) (`FF_GRADSCALER_TRACE_PATH`, `FF_CUDA_GRAPH_RESTORE_BN`, `FF_AMP_FIXED_SCALE`, `FF_AMP_INIT_SCALE` instrumentation), `tests/analysis/test_cuda_graph_gradscale.py` and [tests/shared/test_cuda_graph.py](../../tests/shared/test_cuda_graph.py) (env/trace/fixed-scale coverage), plus [todo/gpu_launch_bound_levers.md](../gpu_launch_bound_levers.md) and [docs/adr/0017-platform-autodetection-per-arch-optimization-policy.md](../../docs/adr/0017-platform-autodetection-per-arch-optimization-policy.md) (decision record). *(Instrumentation removed 2026-06-22: the `cuda_graph_gradscale` harness + the `FF_AMP_FIXED_SCALE` / `FF_AMP_INIT_SCALE` / `FF_GRADSCALER_TRACE_*` knobs were deleted once the FP32+TF32 default (#1311) dropped the `GradScaler` from the default path — vestigial; the conclusion below stands for the opt-in FP16 path. `FF_CUDA_GRAPH_RESTORE_BN` stays.)*
- **What:** The CUDA graph speed knob needed a narrower benchmarkability answer: whether graph-vs-graph runs are stable, whether BatchNorm graph warmup caused the graph/eager split, and whether `GradScaler` scale schedules could be controlled enough to compare graphed runs against eager baselines.
- **Fix:** Added a paired local harness and env-gated diagnostics, then ran RB attention on the RTX 5080 path. Graph-vs-graph was clean; BatchNorm warmup snapshot/restore was inert; dynamic eager-vs-graph `GradScaler` schedules split at step 1. Lower fixed-scale controls (`2048`, `1024`, `512`) did not create a bit-comparable bridge: only `512` completed both arms with identical scale/skip traces, and it still produced an RB attention MAE delta (`graph=4.044157`, `eager=4.060941`).
- **Lesson:** Fixed loss scale removes the scaler-schedule confound but creates a distinct training regime and does not make graph-vs-eager bit-comparable. `FF_CUDA_GRAPH=1` is valid for graphed-vs-graphed local A/Bs after a graphed rebaseline, not for comparison to eager benchmark-history baselines.
