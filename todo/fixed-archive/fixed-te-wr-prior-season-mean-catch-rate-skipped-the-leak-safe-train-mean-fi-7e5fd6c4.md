### [FIXED] TE/WR `prior_season_mean_catch_rate` skipped the leak-safe train-mean fill — rookies zero-filled to a strong negative z-score (RB #390 class)
- **File(s):** [../src/te/features.py](../../src/te/features.py) + [../src/wr/features.py](../../src/wr/features.py) (`fill_nans` carve-outs), audit issues #1290/#1368, 2026-07-01 solve-issues tier PRs.
- **What:** Both positions derive `prior_season_mean_catch_rate` (low-volume-guarded `.where(targets ≥ 0.5)`) and whitelist it in `INCLUDE_FEATURES["prior_season"]`, but it is absent from `_SPECIFIC_FEATURES` — the only column list passed to `fill_nans` — so rookies/no-prior rows fell through to `build_position_features`' catch-all `.fillna(0)`: a raw 0 vs a ~0.60–0.67 train mean → strong negative z-score post-scaler. Live at ~26% of WR rows / ~20% of TE rows. RB fixed the identical defect in #390; TE/WR never mirrored it.
- **Fix:** Mirrored RB's carve-out (append the whitelisted-but-not-specific prior column to the train-mean fill list) in TE and WR `fill_nans`, plus wiring tests pinning the activation preconditions (whitelisted ∧ not-in-specific ∧ emitted-under-this-name). Runtime features → per-position retrain, no splits rebuild.
- **Lesson:** A per-position bug fixed in ONE position (`RB #390`, then DST #856) recurs in the siblings unless the fix is either mirrored everywhere at ship time or lifted into the shared mechanism — this was the 3rd and 4th copy of the same carve-out; the shared-mechanism follow-up is tracked in #1383.
